Khawla Banydomi, AI Instructor

## Publish or update on GitHub Pages

1. Extract the entire ZIP. Copy every extracted file and both `assets` and `audio` folders into the root of your GitHub repository.
2. In GitHub Desktop commit and push the changes. Confirm that GitHub Pages publishes the `main` branch root.
3. Open the published public link. Test in a private browser on a computer and mobile device, including Arabic audio and the final certificate. A compatible HTTPS WebXR browser is needed for immersive headset mode.

## Student instructions

Choose English or Arabic and your grade group. The photo-story mode opens first, and its **Previous**, **Places**, and **Next place** controls let you travel directly without navigating geometry. Look left or right using the photo controls (when the mission card is closed). Select **Explore 3D** to switch to the optional fictional 3D campus. Select the door markers to move between learning stations. **Safe exit** always returns to the calm welcome space. If a situation feels uncomfortable, skip it without losing points.

Listen to Murshid's prerecorded chapter or choose the read-aloud option for the question and choices. The latter uses compatible device speech voices. Narration chapters are included in the archive, and the updated package uses remastered sound with consistent loudness and slightly slower playback. New voice generation was unavailable because the available speech allowance was exhausted; the original two synthetic narrator voices remain in use.

Complete eight safe-choice missions. A gold award notification appears when all eight are complete. Select **Claim award**, **Finish journey**, or **Certificate** to enter a chosen local display name and download the print-ready A4-ratio PNG or open the print view. No real name or personal experience is required, and no names are transmitted.

## Visual provenance and privacy

- The official Khalifa City campus website and its facilities page were reviewed: https://khalifa.icschools.ae/ and https://khalifa.icschools.ae/facilities/. They describe IT and science laboratories, multipurpose rooms, sports courts and other school facilities. Direct asset retrieval from the branch website was blocked.
- The nine photographic story illustrations were AI-generated specifically for this fictional learning journey. They do not depict actual ICS pupils, real bullying incidents, or an exact school layout. The optional 3D campus is schematic.
- The ICS group logo is a publicly available reproduction matching the school logo described in the school's published policies. Image source: https://gludo.org/en/business-directory/4228-international-community-schools-ics-schools- . It is used as school branding, with the explicit branch caption; confirm permission and the preferred campus logo with school leadership before public deployment.

The experience is a teaching aid, not a reporting or counselling system. Review the scenarios with your school's safeguarding lead. Use school procedures for real concerns; students may choose a non-immersive alternative or stop at any point. Do not collect real disclosures through the application.
