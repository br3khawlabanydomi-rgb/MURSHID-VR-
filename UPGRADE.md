# Murshid — A Day at ICS

This is a connected, stylized educational campus inspired by ICS Khalifa City Branch 3. It deliberately uses an invented layout, fictional characters and generic classrooms; it does not represent the actual building plan or actual pupils. The learning tour can be completed without a VR headset.

## What's new

- The eight learning spaces are connected by a central school corridor. Walk freely or tap room entrances and map entries to travel directly. Each room offers a fictional scenario, dialogue, safe choices and feedback. The assembly lights up as missions are completed.
- Murshid (مرشد), the guided virtual companion, replaces Noor. There are 18 locally packaged, AI-generated spoken chapters (nine per language), in Arabic and English, including the introduction. **Hear Murshid** replays the current chapter. **Read question & choices** uses device speech synthesis when available. Narration is synthetic, not a recording of school staff. No microphone or external AI service is required.
- Three grade bands provide simpler choices or more complex fictional contexts. Students can choose a supportive perspective, witness or upstander. All situations are optional and non-graphic.
- The A-Frame 1.6.0 rendering engine is included locally, as are the audio files. The regular browser interface remains available if WebXR hardware is unavailable.

## Updating GitHub

Extract the entire archive and copy all files and the `audio` folder into your existing bullying VR repository root; allow replacement of files from the previous version. Commit and push. Keep `index.html` at the root. With GitHub Desktop, select your repository, choose Repository → Show in Explorer, copy the extracted contents there, commit and push. GitHub Pages must be enabled for the main branch root. Test the published site in a private browser, including Arabic playback and the Safe Exit button.

The campus guide is a structured educational companion, not a live AI chatbot, counsellor, or reporting system. For real concerns, seek a trusted adult and follow school safeguarding procedures. Review the materials with the school safeguarding lead before classroom use.
